About RecipeHub

Good food is better when it's shared.

RecipeHub is a project that was created for individuals that love cooking, recipes, and sharing their delicious creations. Whether they are looking for new recipes to try out, or looking to share their favourite recipe with others, RecipeHub is a website that allows cooks of all ages and abilities to share their knowledge about recipes.

Explore Recipes

Our Purpose

Inspiration for your next meal

RecipeHub was created with the intention of being a basic and enjoyable website for individuals to find and share recipes with others. From English Breakfast to Pasta Bolognese, RecipeHub includes recipes from various recipe categories to allow for the discovery of new recipes or to find the ingredients necessary to prepare a recipe.

Each recipe includes information necessary to prepare the recipe, such as the ingredients, preparation time, cooking time, servings, difficulty level and steps necessary to prepare the recipe.

More than a recipe collection

A place to discover and share

RecipeHub brings home cooks together through recipes, ratings and conversations.

Discover

Browse recipes by category, search by name or ingredient, and explore the favourites of the community for recipe inspiration. From breakfast to dinner and everything in between, RecipeHub features recipes to please every appetite and make finding the next recipe easy for you.

Share

RecipeHub is more than just a website that allows for the viewing of recipes. RecipeHub allows members of the website to become a part of the community by allowing them to upload their own recipes. Each member can access their dashboard to view their uploaded recipes and any linked images of those recipes.
